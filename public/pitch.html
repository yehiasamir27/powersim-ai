Agentic predictive maintenance for the world's industrial power assets.

PowerSim AI catches equipment failures before they happen — an autonomous agent that senses, reasons, and acts on asset health, with an explainable "why" and provable ROI. Deployment-ready architecture, MENA-native wedge, honest simulation-first approach.

01 Problem

Industrial plants lose enormous value to unplanned downtime, while the reliability experts who prevent it are retiring (~1.9M US manufacturing jobs may go unfilled by 2033, per Deloitte). Legacy monitoring drowns operators in false alarms, so maintenance stays reactive. Most predictive-maintenance programs stall in "pilot purgatory" — the blocker is integration and trust, not algorithms.

02 Solution

A continuous sense → think → act agent over a physics-based digital twin. It estimates each asset's health and remaining useful life from sensor fusion, decides what to do, generates the work order, and shows its reasoning at every step — then translates prevented failures into downtime, cost, energy and CO₂ saved against the operator's own numbers.

  • Closes the loop (acts) — not just alerts or a Q&A copilot.
  • Explainable "why" trail — directly counters buyer distrust of black-box scores.
  • Buyer-supplied ROI — no hard-coded headline numbers.
  • Two pillars, one agent — the same engine also classifies waste streams, runs expert-system compliance checks and routes them by a 4R recommender, because degrading equipment produces more contaminated scrap and more regulatory exposure.

04 Research foundation

Our thesis is measured, not asserted. PowerSim AI's design is grounded in "Artificial Intelligence Adoption Intention in Egypt: Effects on Energy Efficiency and Environmental Sustainability" (Khaled Mohamed, AASTMT, June 2026) — a quantitative survey of 120 professionals across Egypt's energy, manufacturing and logistics sectors, analysed in SPSS.

β = 0.761
H1 supported — AI adoption → energy efficiency (R² = 0.579, p < 0.05)
β = 0.636
H2 supported — AI adoption → environmental sustainability (R² = 0.404, p < 0.05)
α = 0.868–0.925
Cronbach's Alpha across both scales — strong reliability

The accompanying literature review — AI in smart grids, predictive maintenance, renewable forecasting, industrial waste management, and Egypt-specific renewable adoption — is the direct source of the waste pillar's four-layer architecture and its expert-system compliance layer. This product operationalizes empirically-validated research, which is a genuine differentiator against AI-energy startups pitching on narrative alone.

The study establishes adoption-intention relationships among surveyed professionals; it does not measure PowerSim AI's own field performance.

07 Business model

Software-first SaaS priced by fleet: Pilot (prove the loop, instrumented ROI) → Plant/Core (whole-plant coverage, OPC-UA/MQTT ingest, ISO 50001 reporting) → Edge/On-prem (air-gap-friendly for data-sensitive operators). Land with a low-friction pilot, expand across sites; final pricing is set with each design partner during that pilot.

08 Traction & roadmap

  • Now: working, tested product with a live explainable demo across predictive maintenance and waste/compliance — plus the empirical study underpinning the thesis.
