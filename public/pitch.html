PowerSim AI

An AI agent that keeps railway power assets healthy.

powersim-ai.vercel.app github.com/yehiasamir27/powersim-ai

Problem

A power failure can stop trains on a whole line. Maintenance often comes too late, and worn equipment quietly wastes energy.

Solution

A digital twin copies each asset in real time. An AI agent senses, thinks and acts every 2 seconds, and explains every decision in plain words.

How it works

What it does

  • Health score and remaining life for 4 rail assets.
  • Opens work orders before a breakdown.
  • Sorts waste, checks the rules and suggests reuse or recycling.
  • Measures energy lost, CO₂ and money saved.

Rail relevance

  • Smart signalling and electrification: monitors the traction transformer, traction motor, cooling and signalling backup power.
  • Grid integration and stability: tracks power factor, voltage drift and energy lost across the network.

Engineering

Python and FastAPI backend with a live WebSocket stream. Optional local LLM through Ollama. A browser version of the engine powers the public demo. 91 automated tests, GitHub Actions CI and Docker.
