How it works

Sense. Think. Act. Every 2 seconds.

1

Sense

Reads temperature, vibration, oil and power quality from every asset.

2

Think

Scores the risk and estimates how long each part will last.

3

Act

Opens a work order or a waste plan and says why in plain words.

What it does

Four jobs, one agent.

Predict failures

Health score and remaining life for every asset.

Explain decisions

Clear reasons in plain words, not a black box.

Cut waste

Sorts waste, checks the rules, suggests reuse or recycling.

Show the impact

Downtime avoided, energy lost, CO₂ and money saved.
