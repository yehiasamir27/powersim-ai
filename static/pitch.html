Agentic predictive maintenance for the world's industrial power assets.

PowerSim AI catches equipment failures before they happen — an autonomous agent that senses, reasons, and acts on asset health, with an explainable "why" and provable ROI. Deployment-ready architecture, MENA-native wedge, honest simulation-first approach.

01 Problem

Industrial plants lose enormous value to unplanned downtime, while the reliability experts who prevent it are retiring (~1.9M US manufacturing jobs may go unfilled by 2033, per Deloitte). Legacy monitoring drowns operators in false alarms, so maintenance stays reactive. Most predictive-maintenance programs stall in "pilot purgatory" — the blocker is integration and trust, not algorithms.

02 Solution

A continuous sense → think → act agent over a physics-based digital twin. It estimates each asset's health and remaining useful life from sensor fusion, decides what to do, generates the work order, and shows its reasoning at every step — then translates prevented failures into downtime, cost, energy and CO₂ saved against the operator's own numbers.

  • Closes the loop (acts) — not just alerts or a Q&A copilot.
  • Explainable "why" trail — directly counters buyer distrust of black-box scores.
  • Buyer-supplied ROI — no hard-coded headline numbers.
